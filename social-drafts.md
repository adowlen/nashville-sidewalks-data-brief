## Held copy - lead with the killer number

Only 69 of Nashville's 1,454 Poor sidewalk segments carry a recorded repair cost. 1,520 Good segments do.

14,938 inventoried segments, 1,401.7 miles (derived). 25.2% are recorded Fair or Poor. District 11 records the highest Poor share at 30.7% (175 of 570); District 21 holds the most Poor segments (212) and the highest mean repair score (113.1). A repair score covers 97.3% of Poor segments; the recorded cost field sums to $59,743,161.85 on just 1,771 segments, 91.6% of it on Good segments - a recorded field sum, not a backlog total.

Full brief, data, and audit: https://github.com/adowlen/nashville-sidewalks-data-brief
